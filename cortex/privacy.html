Cortex privacy policy

Cortex is a private assistant used by one person, the owner of this site, on his own computer.

Google data

When the owner connects his Google account, Cortex reads his Gmail and Google Calendar, and creates email drafts and calendar events, only when he asks it to. It never sends email.

What Cortex reads from Google is processed on the owner's computer. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and not shared with anyone unless the owner himself chooses to send it. Nothing is kept except what the owner chooses to keep, encrypted on his computer.

Cortex's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time by disconnecting Google in Cortex, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
